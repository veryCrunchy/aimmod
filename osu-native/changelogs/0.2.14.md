# AimMod 0.2.14

## Fixed

- Warmup now advances after successful aim drills containing sliders and shows the accuracy from the drill you just finished.
- Slider endings no longer inflate trainer tap counts, timing statistics or reading summaries.

## Improved

- Adaptive difficulty now follows recent practice in fixed drills as well as randomized drills, with a manual option and a preview of the next run.
- Consistent players can progress from their own accuracy level. Timing windows and skill limits increase gradually after repeated steady plays and ease after difficult runs.
- Reaction practice adjusts response time; spinner practice adjusts spin duration. Warmups ease off when needed and gradually return toward your prepared pace.
- Guided practice counts completed low-hit runs and no longer requires a timing-spread measurement to continue.
