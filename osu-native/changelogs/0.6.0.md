# AimMod 0.6.0

- Coaching shows where a play lost its pp: each section is recalculated without its misses and without its 100s and 50s by osu!'s own pp calculator, and the map page leads with what the play is worth, what a full combo would be worth, and the sections to fix first. See [where the pp went](../docs/section-pp.md).
- Practise these sections first starts adaptive practice on the costliest sections, worst first. A section is parked after eight tries while others still need work, and sections that took work come back for review at full speed after a day, then two, then four.
- Statistics sums up replay analysis across every analysed play: unstable rate and early or late bias, overall and per key, pattern trends, and the latest misses, each with its confidence interval and the number of plays it rests on.
- Find a map is cleaner, with unfinished adaptive practice listed first under Continue practising, and My coaching lists maps with adaptive practice above generated practice sets.
- Settings has a new What AimMod reads tab: what AimMod reads, what it writes into osu!, how practice is kept away from scores, and what it shares. See [what AimMod reads](../docs/what-aimmod-reads.md).
- Scores shared to the Hub now report whether the play passed, instead of always reporting a pass.
- Home shows your skillsets: what you play most, the patterns you are strongest and weakest at compared with your own average, and a shortcut to the trainer for your weakest one. See [skillsets](../docs/home-skillsets.md).
- New stream ladder in Trainers: full streams 10 BPM faster after every clean 15-second step, until a miss or uneven timing ends it. It records your highest clean BPM and charts it over time. See [trainers](../docs/trainers.md#stream-ladder).
- Statistics can compare your replays before and since a date you choose, kept per account, and has a 7-day period. Jumps, bursts and streams now appear in its pattern rows.
- Coaching's "Where your misses come from" counts misses inside streams as stream control, and shows whether stream drift is getting more or less frequent.
- PP targets can find maps by skillset: streams, bursts, jumps or sharp angles, measured from each map's own notes, with likely stream maps measured first. Every target shows its main pattern.
- PP targets keep showing your previous targets while a new scan catches up after your skill profile changes, instead of clearing the list.
- PP targets scan about twice as fast after your skill profile changes, and use more of your CPU on machines with many cores.
- On Windows, PP targets can exclude its two scan folders from Microsoft Defender, after explaining why and with Windows' permission, so scans stop waiting for a malware check on every beatmap file. It can be undone in Settings. See [scan speed](../docs/pp-target-scan-speed.md).
- Practice in the beatmap browser opens coaching for the difficulty you selected, instead of a list of the set's difficulties that might not include it.
- Find drills lists the difficulties you played that have no drills yet, with the reason (not analysed yet, no replay saved, analysis failed, or nothing to drill), and opens their coaching page in one click, analysing their replays first if needed. See [find drills](../docs/find-drills.md).
