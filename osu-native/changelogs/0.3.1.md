# AimMod 0.3.1

## Stream trainer star targets

- Fixed the stream generator choosing a longer, slower phrase over a playable faster one, which prevented some star targets from being reached.
- Adaptive streams can now grow into spaced streams within your movement limits, and the target search checks additional flowing paths.
- Generated maps still follow the song's actual notes and must meet the selected star range before starting. Manual settings and skill limits remain respected.
