# AimMod 0.2.13

## Warmup

- Get ready for scores with a new Warmup tab under Trainers.
- Choose 2, 4 or 8 minutes of timing, aim, burst and rhythm drills, each with a different AimMod song.
- Start at a comfortable pace based on recent practice and replay evidence. The next drill eases off after a difficult run, and you can lower the pace yourself.
- Use your connected osu! controls and gameplay settings, review each drill, and pause between runs whenever you need to.
- Keep completed drills in your practice history, then head straight to the beatmap browser.

## Practice

- Recover cleanly when a practice session cannot start or is cancelled during preparation.
