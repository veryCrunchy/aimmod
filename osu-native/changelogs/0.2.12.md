# AimMod 0.2.12

## DT practice

- Speed progression now adapts to your accuracy on each map, with smaller or larger adjustments based on your results.
- Fixed music stopping when switching maps or retrying a DT session.
- Suppressed the osu! menu greeting during embedded practice.

## Your osu! settings

- Practice inherits your selected osu! client's window mode, resolution, playfield scaling, background dim and supported visual settings.
- Slider animations and cursor effects follow your osu! preferences. AimMod's own settings return when practice ends.
- Installed-song and DT practice can show the map's background.

## App updates

- Read what's new from the Home screen before downloading an update.
- Browse versioned changelogs inside AimMod, including bundled release history available offline.
- Release notes stay available while downloading and before restarting.
