# Unreleased

- Home shows your skillsets: what you play most, the patterns you are strongest and weakest at compared with your own average, and a shortcut to the trainer for your weakest one. See [skillsets](../docs/home-skillsets.md).
- New stream ladder in Trainers: full streams 10 BPM faster after every clean 15-second step, until a miss or uneven timing ends it. It records your highest clean BPM and charts it over time. See [trainers](../docs/trainers.md#stream-ladder).
- Statistics can compare your replays before and since a date you choose, kept per account, and has a 7-day period. Jumps, bursts and streams now appear in its pattern rows.
- Coaching's "Where your misses come from" counts misses inside streams as stream control, and shows whether stream drift is getting more or less frequent.
- PP targets can find maps by skillset: streams, bursts, jumps or sharp angles, measured from each map's own notes. Every target shows its main pattern.
