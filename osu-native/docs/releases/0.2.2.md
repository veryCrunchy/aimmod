# AimMod for osu! 0.2.2

- New training preferences enable session sync and public profile sharing by default. Link your account and complete a training session to share your progress.
- Existing sync and privacy choices are preserved. Previously private sessions and existing local practice history remain unchanged.
- Training sync is ready without opening Settings first, and pending sessions retain their sync state across restarts.
- Manage both options in Settings > Account & sharing > Training.
