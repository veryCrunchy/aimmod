# Warmup

Open Trainers > Warmup to prepare a 2, 4 or 8 minute session. Four drills cover steady timing, aim with sliders, short bursts and rhythm changes. The duration describes playing time; count-ins and player-controlled breaks add time. Each drill uses a different AimMod song and the shared embedded osu! player, including connected controls and temporary gameplay/display preferences.

The plan uses recent unassisted practice and account-matched replay evidence. Repeated completed practice at 80% accuracy or better can establish comfortable demand without requiring a 98% score. A conservative fallback is used when evidence is missing. Demand starts below the per-skill ceiling and approaches that ceiling across the session. Notes remain aligned to native song tempo; the generator bounds note density, spacing and aim velocity. Short groups and low complexity keep this separate from progression training.

Results below the player's recent accuracy reference or with more than 5% misses ease subsequent demand. Good results never increase demand beyond the prepared ceiling. Players can lower demand manually, pause between drills or finish early. Cancellation, assisted results and mismatched results do not complete a step. A completed run with no successful taps still eases demand even though it has no timing-spread measurement. Completion is a session summary, not a promise of score readiness.

Completed drills use the existing bounded, per-account practice history with a warmup session/step marker. Warmup runs do not set subsequent warmup or skill-randomizer ceilings. The unfinished plan stays available while the workspace is open; account changes discard it. No extra audio cache or unbounded session file is created. The final action opens the beatmap browser.
