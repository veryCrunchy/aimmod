# Find drills

Coaching's **Find drills** lists one entry per difficulty played in the coaching timeframe that has drills: at least one locally saved replay whose analysis shows misses or a timing problem. The search, mods and star filters apply, and the evidence filter narrows drills further.

## Played without drills

Every other locally played difficulty in the timeframe follows the drills, so none is silently missing, with the reason it has no drills yet:

- **No replay saved:** osu! saved no replay for its plays, and drills are built from replays.
- **Not analysed yet:** its replays have not been analysed. Background analysis covers the newest play of each difficulty first, up to 500 replays.
- **Analysis failed:** every one of its replays failed to analyse this session, usually because the map changed after the plays were set or its file is missing.
- **No misses or timing problems found:** its analysed replays have nothing to drill.

They follow the search, mods and star filters; an evidence filter other than "Any evidence" hides them, since they have no evidence. The count line says how many there are.

Selecting one explains its reason and offers **Open coaching for this difficulty**: its coaching page opens from the newest passed play, otherwise the newest play, so adaptive practice is available. If its replays are not analysed yet, that difficulty's replays are analysed straight away, ahead of the background queue, and the coaching page and drills update when they finish.

**Practice** on a difficulty in the beatmap browser opens the same coaching page for that difficulty directly.
