# Where the pp went

For an analysed osu!standard play, coaching shows which practice sections cost the play the most pp, and the player's best of every section.

## How it is worked out

The map is split into the same sections as adaptive practice. Every number comes from osu!'s own pp calculator, the version AimMod is built with, named on the card. Each what-if is a whole play with exact judgement counts rebuilt from the replay analysis:

- **Misses:** the section with its misses, slider breaks and dropped slider ends taken back. The combo around them is joined up again, exactly as osu! counts combo.
- **100s and 50s:** the section with its 100s and 50s played as 300s. Combo is unchanged.
- **Your best here:** the section played as well as the player's best of it at the same map, mods and speed.

Each number changes only one section. pp is not additive, so the numbers do not add up to a total, and the card says so.

## Section bests

Every analysed play of a map counts, failed plays included: a section counts once the play reached its end. The best of a section is the one with the fewest combo breaks, then the fewest 100s and 50s. Bests are kept per account, map, mods and speed, and only for the same section split.

**Sum of Best** is the pp of one play made of the best of every section, once every section has one.

## Goal

The goal is the most 100s a full combo can have and still be worth more than this play, found with the same calculator.

Failed plays get no pp breakdown, because they have no pp.
