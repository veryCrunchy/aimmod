# Work up to DT

Open Trainers > Mod trainers > Double Time for map-specific speed progression. Skill trainers and Mod trainers keep their own selection and session state when switching tabs. The library groups compact difficulty choices by song, with a separate progress inspector on wide windows and a Change map / Return to selected map path on narrow windows. Shared choice groups, stat tiles, buttons and search controls keep the workspace consistent with the rest of the app. Users choose an installed osu!standard difficulty and play its original objects in the embedded game. The trainer inherits the selected osu! controls, audio offset, input configuration and gameplay appearance through the existing practice player. No Fail is enabled; results are private practice attempts, not submitted osu! scores.

Each account and beatmap hash has independent progress starting at 100%. The first completed run with at least 80% accuracy and at most 1% misses establishes a personal accuracy reference. The target is two accuracy percentage points below that reference, with an 80% floor. Runs meeting the target with at most 1% misses increase speed by one point below 90% accuracy, two points from 90%, or three points from 96%. Any misses, or less than one accuracy point above the personal target, limit the increase to one speed point. There is no fixed 98% gate.

Accuracy below the personal target reduces speed by one point per two accuracy points lost, rounded up and capped at five. More than 1% misses holds speed if accuracy is on target; more than 3% reduces it by three points, and more than 6% or accuracy below 80% reduces it by five. Speed stays between 100% and 150%. Two supported runs at 150% confirm the target. No Fail completion alone cannot earn an increase.

Several recent low-miss runs can raise the reference using their median accuracy. Poor runs never lower it, and one accuracy spike cannot raise an established reference on its own. Existing histories migrate from low-miss attempts at their lowest recorded speed. These are adjustable practice heuristics, not a validated prediction of improvement.

Cancelled, duplicated, stale and assisted results cannot advance progress. Maps need at least 20 objects. Restarting at normal speed clears the clean-run streak while preserving attempt history. The ruleset's Double Time mod applies the requested rate above 100%, preserving the original map and changing approach timing, hit windows and music together. No extra audio or beatmap variants are generated.

Progress is stored locally per account, with up to 100 map entries and 200 recent attempts per map, bounded to 8 MiB. Source audio is held only during preparation and gameplay, capped at 100 MiB. Source leases and temporary tracks are released after the player exits. Private-desktop integration tests cover selecting map difficulties, original circles/sliders/spinners, 100% and 150% gameplay, return to the workspace and saved recovery speed.
