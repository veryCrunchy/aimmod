# AimMod Brand Usage

Write the name as AimMod. Use the supplied vector lettering instead of retyping the wordmark.

## Clear Space and Size

Leave one stem width of clear space around the mark (approximately 50 units on the 320 x 220 master canvas).
Keep wordmarks at least 220 px wide, and Japanese lockups at least 480 px wide. Use the standalone mark or app icon below these sizes.

## Colours

- Mint: #27E4A1
- Ink: #040D09
- Chalk: #C8F3E0
- Deep green: #0D7350
- Sage: #77BD98

Use mint or white on dark backgrounds and black on light backgrounds. Keep strong contrast.

## Artwork

Preserve aspect ratios, cutouts and letter spacing. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects or crop the logo.
SVG files are scalable artwork. PNG files are raster exports. Use the supplied icon sizes for small placements.

## Representation

Use these assets when referring to AimMod. Do not imply an official partnership or endorsement, or impersonate the project. Keep third-party game branding separate.
